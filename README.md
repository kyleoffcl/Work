# Jubilee International Mining Company Limited — Website

A modern, single-page website for **Jubilee International Mining Company Limited**,
Africa's finest direct seller of certified 99.9% pure gold, headquartered in
Kampala, Uganda. The site is focused on **gold selling** — gold bars, nuggets and
bulk institutional supply — styled in the company's original palette (white with
bright-yellow accents, sage-green highlights, dark hero and black footer), with
scroll-reveal animations, animated stat counters and percentage rings.

## Sections
- Hero with CSS-rendered gold bar and buyer trust points
- Trust strip (Bank of Uganda, Ministry of Energy & Mineral Development, ISO 9001:2015)
- Government Relations / Sustainability / Traceable Sourcing pillars
- Who We Are + animated statistics
- **Buy Gold**: cast bars, nuggets & raw gold, bulk supply + 4-step buying process
- Goal / Vision / Mission
- Services (gold sales & supply, assaying, transaction handling, gemstones, consultancy…)
- Government & institutional partnerships + partners (Palm Refinery Dubai, Light Moon Group)
- Community & mining partnerships (Mubende / MUMA)
- Capability meters, track record, FAQ
- Contact: Kampala HQ, Dubai and Kenya offices + inquiry form
  (**info@jubileeintlminingco.com**)

## Run locally
Just open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then visit http://localhost:8000
```

## Files
- `index.html` — markup
- `styles.css` — design tokens, layout, animations
- `script.js` — nav, scroll reveal, counters, rings, form handling

No build step, images or dependencies required — all visuals are pure CSS.
