Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

I've never done any martial arts. Can I really start?

Absolutely — most of our members started with zero experience. Our Beginner Fundamentals program is designed exactly for first-timers, and your free week includes a 1-on-1 orientation so you'll never feel lost.

Do I need to be in shape before I join?

No. You get in shape by training. Go at your own pace — jiu-jitsu is for every body type and fitness level. Many members join specifically to lose weight and build conditioning.

What should I bring to my first class?

Just comfortable athletic clothes and a water bottle. We'll lend you a clean gi for your first session at no cost. Bare feet on the mat, please!

Is jiu-jitsu safe? Will I get hurt?

Safety is our top priority. Classes are structured and supervised, you control your own intensity, and we cultivate a respectful training culture. It's far safer than most contact sports.

How old does my child need to be?

We welcome kids from age 4 and up, grouped by age and skill so every child trains with peers at their level.

Is there really no contract?

Correct. All memberships are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. Plus, your first month is backed by our 30-Day Happiness Guarantee.
