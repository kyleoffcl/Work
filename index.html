How do I book a tour?+

Pick a tour and tap Book Now, or message us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm instantly and send your voucher by email — no payment needed until you're ready.

Yes — most tours include complimentary pickup and drop-off from hotels and residences across Dubai. Just share your location when booking.

Absolutely. Most tours offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure. We'll happily reschedule at no extra cost.

Which payment methods do you accept?+

We accept all major credit/debit cards, bank transfer, cash and digital wallets. Pay online or in person — whatever suits you.
