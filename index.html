FAQ

Questions frequently asked by clients all over the globe

Jubilee International Mining Company Limited sells certified, high-purity gold — here's what buyers ask us most.

What does Jubilee International Mining Company Limited sell?

We sell certified 99.9% pure gold bars, gold nuggets and raw gold, plus bulk gold supply for dealers and institutions. We also offer gemstone testing, transaction handling and mineral consultancy.

How do I buy gold from you?

Contact our sales team with the quantity and form of gold you need. We'll provide assay documentation and a formal offer, arrange secure payment and transaction handling, then deliver worldwide or arrange collection.

What is the minimum amount of gold I can purchase?

We serve buyers of every scale — from single bars to multi-kilo institutional orders. Contact us and we'll structure an order that fits your needs.

Is Jubilee International Mining Company Limited licensed?

Yes. We are a government-designated and licensed gold supplier, serving as an official supplier to the Bank of Uganda and partnering with the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Development.

How is purity guaranteed?

Every product is independently assayed and certified 99.9% pure, then sealed and serial-numbered. Full documentation accompanies every sale.

Do you sell gold internationally?

Yes — we serve clients globally and handle both local and international transactions, with insured export and delivery worldwide.

What are your payment terms?

We support secure, verifiable payment methods with transaction handling that protects both parties. Terms are agreed per contract before any deal proceeds.

Is your gold responsibly sourced?

Yes. We source through licensed and artisanal partnerships across Uganda — including MUMA miners in Mubende — with eco-friendly practices and full traceability from mine to market.
